# Incident, Injury & Near-Miss Reporting Protocol

All accidents, injuries, environmental spills, and near-miss occurrences must be reported immediately, regardless of perceived severity.

## Reporting Timeline

1. **Immediate Notification (< 15 Minutes)**: Notify your immediate supervisor and the on-duty first aider.
2. **Medical Treatment First**: Attend to any injured individuals before commencing administrative paperwork.
3. **Formal Incident Report (< 24 Hours)**: Complete the Digital Safety Incident Form in the management portal.
4. **Investigation & Action Plan (< 7 Days)**: Safety committee convenes to determine root causes and implement preventive measures.

> [!NOTE]
> Reporting near-misses allows our organization to eliminate hazards before an injury occurs. We enforce a strict non-punitive, just-culture reporting framework.
