# Hazard Identification & PPE Matrix

Hazard control follows the established Hierarchy of Controls: Elimination, Substitution, Engineering Controls, Administrative Controls, and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).

## Mandatory PPE Matrix by Area

| Work Zone | Safety Boots | Eye Protection | High-Vis Vest | Hearing Protection | Cut-Resistant Gloves |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Offices & Common Areas** | Optional | Optional | No | No | No |
| **Warehouse & Logistics** | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory | Optional | When handling goods |
| **Fabrication & Assembly** | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory (Level 4+) |
| **Chemical Storage** | Mandatory | Chemical Splash Goggles | Mandatory | Optional | Nitrile / Chemical Gauntlet |
