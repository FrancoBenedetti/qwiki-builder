# Health & Safety Policy & Golden Safety Rules

Our company is firmly dedicated to providing a safe, healthy working environment for all employees, contractors, visitors, and neighboring communities. Zero harm is our fundamental target.

## Golden Safety Rules

1. **Stop Work Authority**: Every employee has the authority and moral obligation to stop any task they believe is unsafe without fear of reprisal.
2. **Never Bypass Safety Guards**: Interlocks, light curtains, and physical barriers must never be overridden or removed during operation.
3. **Wear Required PPE**: Appropriate gear must be worn at all times in designated operational zones.
4. **Lockout/Tagout Before Servicing**: All mechanical, electrical, and pneumatic lines must be zero-energized before service.
5. **No Mobile Devices Near Machinery**: Stay focused and avoid distractions when operating moving machinery or vehicles.
