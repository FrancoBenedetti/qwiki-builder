| **Owner** | Shift Operations | **Approver** | Plant Manager |

## 1. Operational Overview

A structured face-to-face handover at shift changeover guarantees seamless continuation of running processes and zero loss of safety information.

## 2. Shift Change Protocol

1. **15 Minutes Prior to Shift End**: Outgoing operator completes current logbook entries, noting raw material consumption, yield, and mechanical anomalies.
2. **Joint Walkthrough**: Outgoing and incoming operators inspect physical work cell, scrap bins, and safety guards together.
3. **Electronic Sign-off**: Both parties sign the electronic shift transfer form on the terminal.
