| Document ID | SOP-001 | Version | 2.0 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effective Date** | 2026-11-01 | **Review Cycle** | Annual |
| **Owner** | Operations Excellence | **Approver** | Head of Quality |

## 1. Purpose & Scope

Defines the universal format, numbering convention, peer review, and authorization workflow for creating or modifying company Standard Operating Procedures.

## 2. Procedure Numbering Standard

- `SOP-000` to `SOP-099`: Administrative & Governance Procedures
- `SOP-100` to `SOP-199`: Operations & Manufacturing Procedures
- `SOP-200` to `SOP-299`: Quality & Laboratory Inspection
- `SOP-300` to `SOP-399`: Information Technology & Security

## 3. Mandatory Document Structure

Every official SOP must contain:
1. Title & Header Control Block.
2. Purpose & Operational Scope.
3. Precautions, Warnings & PPE Requirements.
4. Step-by-Step Execution Sequence.
5. Verification, Sign-off & Records Archival.
