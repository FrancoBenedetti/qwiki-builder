# Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Find quick answers to common questions and escalation contacts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I share a document with an external partner?
Click the **Share** icon on any published page to obtain a cryptographically secure, read-only direct link.

### Q: Why do I see a "Review" banner on a page?
Documents in review have not received final managerial approval. They remain watermarked until signed off.

### Q: Who do I contact for permission changes?
Reach out to your system administrator or open a ticket through the IT helpdesk.
