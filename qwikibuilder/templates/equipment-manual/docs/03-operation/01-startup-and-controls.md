# Startup Sequences & Operator Controls

This section covers the standard operating routines for day-to-day production.

## Pre-Start Inspection Checklist

- [ ] Workspace around machine is clean and free of tripping hazards.
- [ ] Emergency stop pushbuttons are pulled out and ready.
- [ ] Protective guards and inspection hatches are closed and securely fastened.
- [ ] Lubricant reservoir level is above the minimum indicator line.
- [ ] Supply air pressure gauge reads between 6.0 and 7.5 bar.

## Normal Startup Procedure

1. Energize the main disconnect switch.
2. Wait 45 seconds for the PLC and HMI to boot to the Home Screen.
3. Verify that the indicator beacon illuminates steady amber (Standby).
4. Select the desired operating recipe from the recipe selection menu.
5. Press and hold the green **CYCLE START** button for 2 seconds.
6. The alert buzzer will sound for 3 seconds prior to mechanical movement.
