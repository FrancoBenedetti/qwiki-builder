# Safety Precautions & Lockout/Tagout Procedures

> [!CAUTION]
> **DANGER: HIGH VOLTAGE AND PINCH POINTS**
> Failure to follow these safety procedures may result in severe personal injury or death.

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

All personnel within the perimeter must wear:
- Steel-toed safety boots (EN ISO 20345 / ASTM F2413).
- Impact-resistant safety glasses with side shields (ANSI Z87.1).
- Hearing protection in environments exceeding 85 dBA.
- High-visibility safety vest in production areas.

## Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Protocol

Before performing cleaning, lubrication, or mechanical adjustments:

1. **Notify Affected Operators**: Inform area supervisors that the equipment is being shut down.
2. **Normal Shutdown**: Depress the cycle stop button on the HMI and wait for motors to come to a complete standstill.
3. **Isolate Energy**: Rotate the main rotary disconnect switch to `OFF (0)`.
4. **Apply Lock & Tag**: Attach your assigned personal padlock and danger tag to the hasp.
5. **Verify Zero Energy**: Attempt to restart the equipment using the HMI start button to confirm isolation. Return switch to stop.
